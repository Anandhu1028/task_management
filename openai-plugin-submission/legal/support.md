## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I connect TaskFlow to ChatGPT?

In ChatGPT, locate the TaskFlow integration or connector, select **Connect**, and complete the OAuth 2.1 login and consent flow with your TaskFlow account credentials.

### Why can't I see tasks from other teams or companies?

TaskFlow enforces strict multi-tenant and role-based access control. Employees only view their assigned tasks, Team Leaders view department tasks, and Managers/CEOs view their assigned company records.

### How do I disconnect ChatGPT or revoke access?

You can disconnect the integration directly in your ChatGPT account settings (Connected apps → TaskFlow → Disconnect), or visit:
https://tracker-manage.focuzacademy.in/data-deletion

### What scopes does the ChatGPT integration use?

- `tasks:read` — view tasks and subtasks
- `reports:read` — view reports and analytics
- `tasks:write` — create tasks and submit daily updates
- `offline_access` — maintain background connection with refresh tokens

### How do I request a company account or invitation?

Contact admin@focuzacademy.in with your name, organization, and intended role.
